Data Deletion Instructions for Shredzilla

Last Updated: May 27, 2025

At Shredzilla, we respect your right to control your personal data. This document provides instructions on how you can request the deletion of your data from our systems.

1. Data You Can Delete

You can request the deletion of the following types of data associated with your Shredzilla account:

  • Account Information: Your username, email address, profile picture (if any), and other registration details.
  • Fitness and Health Data: All workout logs, exercise history, recorded sets, reps, weights, body measurements, and goals you have entered into the app.
  • User-Generated Content: Any custom workout plans, notes, or other content you have created within the app.

Note: Some anonymized or aggregated data that does not personally identify you may be retained for analytical purposes. Additionally, data required for legal or regulatory compliance may be retained as mandated by law.

2. How to Request Data Deletion

To request the deletion of your data, please follow these steps:

Method 1: In-App Deletion (If Available)

  1. Open the Shredzilla app on your device.
  2. Navigate to the 'Account' or 'Settings' section.
  3. Look for an option such as 'Delete Account', 'Manage Data', or 'Privacy Settings'.
  4. Follow the on-screen prompts to confirm your identity and request data deletion.
  5. You may receive a confirmation email once the process is initiated or completed.

Method 2: Email Request

If an in-app option is not available, or if you prefer, you can request data deletion by sending an email to our support team:

  1. Compose an email to: kareem209907@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: "Data Deletion Request - Shredzilla"
  3. In the body of the email, please include the following information to help us identify your account and process your request efficiently:
    • Your full name (if provided during registration).
    • The email address associated with your Shredzilla account.
    • Your Shredzilla username (if applicable).
    • A clear statement that you are requesting the deletion of your personal data.

Verification: To protect your privacy and security, we may need to verify your identity before processing your data deletion request. We may contact you to request additional information for this purpose.

3. What Happens After You Request Deletion

  • Processing Time: We will process your request within 30 days of receiving it and verifying your identity.
  • Confirmation: We will notify you once your data has been deleted.
  • Consequences of Deletion: Once your data is deleted, it cannot be recovered. You will lose access to your Shredzilla account and all associated data, including workout history and progress. If you wish to use Shredzilla again in the future, you will need to create a new account.
  • Data Backups: Please note that your data may remain in our backup systems for a limited period (e.g., up to 90 days) after deletion from our active systems, as part of our standard data backup and disaster recovery procedures. After this period, it will be permanently erased from backups as well.
