## 2. What the app provides

DotShredzilla helps you log sets and workouts, follow ready-made programs, run
guided workouts with a rest timer, and track personal records, workout history
and body measurements. Some features require an account, an internet
connection, Android permissions or compatible device capabilities. Sets logged
without a connection are kept on the device and synchronized later. Features
can vary with the app version and device.

Check the exercise, weight, reps and other details you enter. Charts, records,
suggestions and insights are calculated from that information and are only as
accurate as what you log.
