DotShredzilla keeps your training log, workout plans and progress in one place. Record sets as you train, follow a session and look back at the details later.

LOG YOUR WORKOUT
Record reps, weights and sets with support for kilograms, pounds, plate counts and bodyweight exercises. Keep warm-up, drop, rest-pause and failure sets clear in your log. A built-in rest timer helps you move between sets.

TRAIN WITH A PLAN
Create your own exercise lists or start from a ready-made training program. Follow a guided workout, see previous-session details and save a session summary when you finish. Supersets and tempo notes help you record how you trained.

SEE YOUR TRAINING HISTORY
Review exercises, total sets, session volume and duration. Find personal records and view charts of your logged training. Track your weekly workout goal and activity over time.

KEEP MEASUREMENTS TOGETHER
Record body measurements and bodyweight, review earlier entries and view changes over time. Choose metric or imperial units to suit your routine.

YOUR ACCOUNT AND YOUR DATA
Sign in with Google or email and password. Log sets offline; cloud synchronization resumes when a connection is available. Choose a light or dark theme. Native app labels are currently in English.

You can delete your account and its associated data from the app settings. For an external deletion request, follow the account deletion instructions linked below. Ownership verification is required; verified requests are handled within 30 days after verification as described in the privacy policy.

DotShredzilla is a workout logging tool. Training suggestions are general fitness guidance, not medical advice. The app contains ads.
